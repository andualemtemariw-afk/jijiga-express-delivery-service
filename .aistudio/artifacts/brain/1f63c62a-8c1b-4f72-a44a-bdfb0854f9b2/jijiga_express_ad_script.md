# 🎬 JIJIGA EXPRESS — Commercial Video Production Script
**Project:** Jijiga Express Hyperlocal Super-App Commercial  
**Target Audience:** Residents, families, merchants, students, and businesses in Jijiga (Somali Region, Ethiopia)  
**Duration:** 60 Seconds  
**Format:** 16:9 (Landscape) + 9:16 (Reels/TikTok cutdown ready)  
**Style:** Cinematic, authentic Horn of Africa aesthetic, high-energy documentary-commercial hybrid  
**Languages:** Somali (Af-Soomaali) & Amharic (አማርኛ)  
**Optimization:** AI Video Engines (Runway Gen-3 Alpha, Sora, Kling, Luma Dream Machine, Midjourney v6 prompts) & AI Voice (ElevenLabs)

---

## 🎵 Sound Design & Music Direction
- **Music Track:** Starts with a soulful acoustic Somali Oud melody fused with an upbeat Afro-electronic rhythmic beat, building up to an inspiring, modern tech tempo.
- **SFX:** Morning call to prayer distant echo, bustling Taiwan Market chatter, motorcycle engine hum, phone notification chime, tamper seal snap, satisfied smile and door chime.

## 🛠️ AI Video Generation Guide (Instructions for AI Video Creators)

| Tool | Recommended Workflow |
|---|---|
| **Runway Gen-3 Alpha / Kling AI / Sora** | Copy each scene's `AI Video Prompt` verbatim into text-to-video mode. Use prompt weighting for `photorealistic, Ethiopian street life, cinematic daylight`. |
| **Midjourney v6 + Luma Dream Machine** | Generate keyframes in Midjourney using prompts with `--ar 16:9 --v 6.1 --style raw`, then animate with Luma camera moves (e.g. `drone pan down`, `low angle tracking drive`). |
| **ElevenLabs (Voiceover)** | Use warm, energetic African voice profiles (e.g. "Adam" or "Fin" for Somali; "Marcus" or "Salem" for Amharic). Set stability to 55% for dynamic, persuasive inflection. |
| **CapCut / Premiere Pro** | Sync voiceover tracks to 8-10 second scene pacing. Add bilingual subtitles with bold colored tags: Emerald (#10b981) for Somali, Amber/Gold (#f59e0b) for Amharic. |
